# Image rules — owner instruction, 1 November 2026

- People in page images are Pakistani. No white or other foreign people on ordinary pages.
- Exception: marriage, court-marriage and online-Nikah pages may show a foreign spouse marrying a Pakistani, because the firm arranges such marriages. Owner's examples (1 Nov 2026): American, British, Argentinian, Chinese, Moroccan, Tunisian, Egyptian, Libyan, Chadian, Saudi and other Arab, Iranian, Filipina, Sri Lankan, and white or black spouses generally. Online Nikah pages especially, as many such Nikahs are with foreign spouses.
- Flags: only the Pakistani flag, if any flag appears. Never another country's flag.
- Court-marriage pages never show a courtroom or court building. Show the office: a maulana performing the Nikah, the Nikah Nama form being filled in, or an affidavit being prepared.
- No blurred faces; show people from behind or as hands. No readable text, numbers, dates or logos.
