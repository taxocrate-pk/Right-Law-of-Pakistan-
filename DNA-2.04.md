# DNA Strategic Policy 2.04 — Current Editorial Rules

User instruction added 2 October 2026:
- Every money page must include named lawyer profiles with genuine portraits, verified professional designations and concise introductions relevant to the service.
- Include one Advocate Supreme Court and one Advocate High Court. Place the senior Supreme Court advocate first.
- Use consistent card styling and genuine professional profile or official directory links where available. Label a general directory accurately; do not imply it is an individual verified entry.
- Refine ranking pages by improving and adding content. Remove only confirmed duplicate content; preserve other existing useful content, URL, title and intent unless the user separately authorizes a change.
- Never publish incidental meeting/client conversation.

Implementation begins with the two succession routes, income-tax return filing, corporate/tax services and Karachi company registration. Remaining money pages require page-by-page application and verification; this document is not a claim that all pages are completed.


## Text And Background Contrast — 4 October 2026

- Text colour must never be the same as, similar to, or too close to its background colour.
- Always use light text on dark backgrounds and dark text on light backgrounds.
- On dark blue hero sections, use white or a clearly contrasting near-white colour for all headings, paragraphs, links and emphasis.
- Apply this rule to headings, body text, buttons, links, captions, navigation and hover/focus states. Check inherited theme styles and mobile layouts before marking a page complete.
- Verify the rendered colour contrast; a saved CSS rule alone is not visual verification.


## Version And Scope

DNA 2.04 supersedes DNA 2.03. Carry forward all previously authorised DNA 2.03 requirements; this file records the new amendments and does not replace the full earlier policy with only these amendments.

## City Money Pages — Local Service Areas

- Every city-specific money page must include a clearly labelled block of relevant areas or neighbourhoods in that city, using correct, verified spellings.
- Cover the city’s principal areas appropriately; Karachi pages should include a substantial block of about 50 relevant service areas where suitable.
- Make area names clearly visible and bold. Use a readable responsive grid or list.
- Describe these as areas served or areas from which enquiries are accepted. Do not invent offices, client histories or proximity claims for every area.
- Give each actual office address on a separate line. Karachi pages should list the Karachi offices, including DHA Phase 7 and Gulistan-e-Johar Blocks 17 and 18 where relevant. Keep unrelated cities out of city-specific address blocks.

## Headings

- One H1 per rendered page, counting theme-generated titles. Do not repeat the H1 in the hero or the following H2.
- Use connected keywords and distinct meanings in H2/H3 headings, with each word’s first letter capitalised.

- Routine Nikah, online marriage and matrimonial service pages must not use Mohsin Ali Shah or Akhtar Rizvi portraits. This exception supersedes the earlier mandatory Supreme Court/High Court profile pairing for those pages.
- Use relevant local team profiles: Karachi — Shankar Lal Kataria, Mohsin Ali Mirani, Zaheer Ashraf Qazi, Sobia Mohsin; Islamabad/Rawalpindi — Kashif Mumtaz, Advocate High Court; Lahore — Junaid Kahloon.

Senior profiles on property/family disputes and appeals must reflect the actual assignment. Akhtar Rizvi and Fayyaz are relevant for senior appellate representation.
