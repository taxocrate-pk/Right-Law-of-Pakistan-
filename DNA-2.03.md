## Text And Background Contrast — 4 October 2026

- Text colour must never be the same as, similar to, or too close to its background colour.
- Always use light text on dark backgrounds and dark text on light backgrounds.
- On dark blue hero sections, use white or a clearly contrasting near-white colour for all headings, paragraphs, links and emphasis.
- Apply this rule to headings, body text, buttons, links, captions, navigation and hover/focus states. Check inherited theme styles and mobile layouts before marking a page complete.
- Verify the rendered colour contrast; a saved CSS rule alone is not visual verification.
